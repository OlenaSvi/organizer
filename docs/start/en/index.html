Organizer: getting started

A personal organizer without guilt. There is no word “overdue” here and no red as a reproach. The app lives at olenasvi.github.io/organizer — on any device with an internet connection. Seven steps, and from there it explains itself.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Open the address, press Create account, type your email and choose a password. No confirmation email to wait for — you are in right away. Sign in with the same email and password on your laptop, iPad or any computer: your tasks are the same everywhere.

  2. 2

    Look around the “Today” screen

    On the left — today's tasks; on the right — routines and the day's events. At the bottom of the task column is “Suggested”: the app proposes what to take on and explains, in words, why that one.

    A day holds five tasks, not forty. That's not a limit, it's honesty: that's how much actually gets done.

  3. 3

    Delete the examples and add your own

    The first ten tasks are examples, there to show what the app can do. Feel free to delete them. Your own go in through ＋ Add at the top right: name, kind, area, deadline. A name and Enter is enough — the rest can be filled in later.

    Five kinds: task, routine (repeats on chosen days), appointment (date and time), idea (no deadline, asks nothing of you) and reminder (nothing to do — just remember: a birthday, a passport expiry).

  4. 4

    Take a task into the day — and tick it off

    In “Suggested”, press take it. Done it — the tick to the left of the task. Didn't get to it — nothing happens: tomorrow it's back in the suggestions, no reproach.

    A long task you'll be working on for weeks can be Take it on: it gets a purple dot and stops dropping out of the day.

  5. 5

    See the same tasks from other angles

    The tabs at the top: Today — what I'm doing; Calendar — appointments and deadlines by day; Map — your whole life as branches by area; Matrix — important and urgent; List — everything, with search. Same tasks, different angles — pick the one that helps right now.

  6. 6

    Make it yours

    The gear at the top right. That's where areas, places and people live — rename “Country 1” to your country, add your people. Also the day's limit (3, 5 or 7 tasks), the theme and the language (English or Russian).

  7. 7

    Something's off — say so

    At the bottom of Settings there's a Report a problem button. It opens a ready-made email: the version and the screen are already filled in, just add a few words about what happened. The same link sits on the sign-in screen, in case signing in is the problem.

The one rule The app never scolds. A skipped routine is simply not ticked. A past deadline looks as calm as a future one. Your tasks are seen by you alone.
